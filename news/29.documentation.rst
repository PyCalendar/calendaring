The design documents no longer say that recurring-task completion works like caldav's: ``task.complete()`` on a task with an ``RRULE`` raises ``UnsupportedError`` on every backend until edit-icalendar has it, with the CalDAV escape hatch described meanwhile.  They also say that on CalDAV an item returned by a write has ``native`` set to ``None`` until it is read back, and list edit-icalendar under the roadmap's risks.
