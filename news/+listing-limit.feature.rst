New capability ``listing.complete`` and error ``ListingLimitError``: a server that cuts a long listing at a limit of its own (Stalwart, at its ``WebDav.maxResults``, 2000 objects by default) declares ``listing.complete`` unsupported, and a listing that hits the limit raises ``ListingLimitError`` instead of a ``BackendError`` where the server answers it with a 507.  None of the listing is returned.
